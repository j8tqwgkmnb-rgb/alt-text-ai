## App Name
Alt Text AI — Accessibility Compliance

## Tagline (80 chars max)
AI-generated alt text for product images. WCAG compliant in one click.

## Detailed Description

ADA accessibility lawsuits are up 27% year over year, with e-commerce stores making up 77% of targets. Shopify stores account for 32% of these lawsuits — and 89% of Shopify stores have missing image alt text. The EU European Accessibility Act is now enforceable with fines up to 4% of revenue.

Alt Text AI fixes this in one click.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Install the app — no configuration needed
2. See every product image in your store with its alt text status
3. Click "Generate" on any image, or "Generate All" to fix everything at once
4. AI analyzes each image and writes descriptive, accurate alt text
5. Alt text is saved directly to your Shopify product data

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Unlike SEO-focused alt text tools that stuff keywords into descriptions, Alt Text AI writes alt text the way accessibility guidelines require — describing what is visually shown in the image. This is what WCAG 2.1 Success Criterion 1.1.1 demands, and what courts evaluate in ADA lawsuits.

- AI vision analyzes the actual image content (color, shape, material, context)
- Descriptions are concise (under 125 characters) and screen-reader friendly
- No keyword stuffing — compliance-first, not SEO-first
- One-click bulk generation for your entire catalog

- Per-image status badges (Has alt text / Missing)
